# AI-assisted contributions

AI tools may be used for exploration, implementation, tests, or documentation, but the human contributor remains responsible for every submitted line.

Pull requests using substantial AI assistance must disclose the tools used, identify generated or transformed areas, describe validation performed, and confirm that no private code, credentials, customer data, or incompatible third-party material was supplied to the model or copied into the repository.

Maintainers review AI-assisted and manually written changes under the same correctness, security, licensing, and test standards. Unreviewed bulk generation, fabricated benchmarks, invented users, and unverifiable claims are not accepted.
